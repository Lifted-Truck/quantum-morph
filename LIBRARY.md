[L0004] Pin assignments, not variates — Math.log is not bit-portable | canonical | added: 2026-08-18 | tags: morph-engine, max-js | lesson: `Math.log` (hence every Gumbel variate `g = −ln(−ln u)`) is NOT bit-identical across platforms or V8 builds. Goldens that compare raw float noise tables pass on the machine that generated them and fail everywhere else; goldens that compare ARGMAX RESULTS survive, because argmax margins are ~15 orders of magnitude larger than libm drift. Pin what is deterministic by construction (integer PRNG state, and `u = (uint32+0.5)/2^32`, an exact division) and use a tight ULP tolerance only where libm is in the path. Corollary for the device: Max's `v8` is a third V8 build, so any device-side golden must compare assignments, never variates. | evidence: CI red 2026-08-05→2026-08-18 on ubuntu-x64 against darwin-arm64 goldens — 55/59 pass, and all 4 failures were raw-float table comparisons while every assignment golden passed (GitHub Actions run 32093558058). | falsifier: a CI run on a third platform where the float tables match bit-for-bit, which would mean the drift came from something other than libm. | supersedes: —

[L0005] Local green is not CI green — check the runner, not your machine | canonical | added: 2026-08-18 | tags: morph-engine | lesson: A green `./verify fast` locally is evidence about ONE platform. [L0006] A transient, unreproducible RED can be another process writing in your tree | canonical | added: 2026-08-18 | tags: morph-engine | lesson: `./verify fast` went red once and green a minute later on an identical tree — no checkout, no stash, nothing untracked afterwards, and CI green on the same commit. [L0007] A declaration is a claim about a copy, a snapshot, or one platform — test the effective behaviour | canonical | added: 2026-08-18 | tags: harness | lesson: This repo hit the same trap three times in three weeks, each time invisible to the check that was supposed to catch it. (1) `.gitattributes` passed the kit presence check because it existed on DISK while being untracked, so its LF guarantee was true of one machine and false of the repo. (2) `./verify fast` was green LOCALLY for 13 days while CI was red — a claim about one platform heard as a claim about the code (see L0005).
