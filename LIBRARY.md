[L0003] QM-0 §10.4 needs a boundary carve-out or it fails on correct code | candidate | added: 2026-07-24 | tags: morph-engine | lesson: Acceptance test §10.4 ("T=0.02 produces the nearest corner for every slot at every position not exactly on a boundary") reads as near-universal, but the exclusion is doing heavy lifting: at (0.5,0.5) all four weights are 0.25, and on any x=0.5 or y=0.5 line two weights tie, so the log-weight terms cancel and the frozen noise alone decides the argmax — correct behaviour that looks exactly like a temperature bug. [L0002] LOM write-rate throttling is a solved-by-others problem, not a hypothetical | candidate | added: 2026-07-24 | tags: m4l-lom | lesson: Continuous morph devices in Live saturate the Live API — J74 Morph's own FAQ documents that morphing "keeps on sending lots of parameter values in a matter of milliseconds" and ships a user-facing *Sample Interval* control purely to throttle it and cut CPU. [L0004] Pin assignments, not variates — Math.log is not bit-portable | canonical | added: 2026-08-18 | tags: morph-engine, max-js | lesson: `Math.log` (hence every Gumbel variate `g = −ln(−ln u)`) is NOT bit-identical across platforms or V8 builds. Goldens that compare raw float noise tables pass on the machine that generated them and fail everywhere else; goldens that compare ARGMAX RESULTS survive, because argmax margins are ~15 orders of magnitude larger than libm drift. Pin what is deterministic by construction (integer PRNG state, and `u = (uint32+0.5)/2^32`, an exact division) and use a tight ULP tolerance only where libm is in the path. Corollary for the device: Max's `v8` is a third V8 build, so any device-side golden must compare assignments, never variates. | evidence: CI red 2026-08-05→2026-08-18 on ubuntu-x64 against darwin-arm64 goldens — 55/59 pass, and all 4 failures were raw-float table comparisons while every assignment golden passed (GitHub Actions run 32093558058). | falsifier: a CI run on a third platform where the float tables match bit-for-bit, which would mean the drift came from something other than libm. | supersedes: —
